# Repository conventions

Use compact, precise prose, standard mathematical terminology, descriptive
filenames, and GitHub-rendered mathematics. Explain assumptions and physical
meaning before implementation details. Follow CONTRIBUTING.md.

Do not add unfinished proofs, new axioms, unsafe declarations, or native
decision procedures. Verify compilation and transitive theorem axioms.
Derive the integration-by-parts identity from analytic hypotheses; do not
assume the desired expectation identity or its unnormalized equivalent.

Keep theorem statements, proof guide, and verification report consistent.
Preserve attribution. Keep documentation focused on definitions, hypotheses,
proofs, examples, and verification.
Use main for checked work. Leave publication to the owner in GitHub Desktop.
